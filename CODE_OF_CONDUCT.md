# Code of Conduct

Orbit follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to project spaces, issue and pull-request discussions, reviews, and maintainer-run community channels.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through GitHub. Do not use public issues for conduct or security reports. Maintainers will acknowledge concerns, protect privacy where possible, investigate fairly, communicate outcomes to involved parties, and prohibit retaliation against good-faith reporters.
